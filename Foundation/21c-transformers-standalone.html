4.5.6 Transformers

See how transformers step voltage up and down across the national grid — the Vp/Vs = Np/Ns equation, why high-voltage transmission saves enormous amounts of energy, and how a transformer uses electromagnetic induction to work its magic.

3Notes

Core

A transformer changes an a.c. voltage. Three parts: primary coil (input Vp), secondary coil (output Vs), soft iron core linking them magnetically (no direct electrical connection). Step-up (more turns on secondary) increases voltage/decreases current; step-down does the opposite.

Vp/Vs = Np/Ns. Example (21.1): 800 primary turns, 16,000 secondary, 25 kV in → Vs=25,000×16,000/800 = 500 kV.

The national grid steps voltage up (400 kV+) for transmission, then down (e.g. 230 V) for use. Higher voltage → smaller current for the same power (P=IV) → much less energy wasted heating cables.

Supplement

A.c. in the primary makes an alternating field in the core, inducing a current in the secondary — d.c. gives an unchanging field, so induces nothing (transformers need a.c.).

Cable power loss = I2R. Example (21.2): 20kW at 5kV through 20Ω: I=4A, loss=320W. Stepped up to 20kV: I=1A, loss=20W — 16× less waste for 4× the voltage.
IpVp = IsVs (~100% efficient). Example (21.3): 9V/3A output from 230V mains → Ip=27/230≈0.12A.

4Key terms

Pronunciation reference: British Received Pronunciation (RP). /ə/ = the soft "uh" sound in an unstressed syllable — this sound doesn't exist in Bahasa Indonesia.

Transformernoun
A device that increases or decreases the voltage of an a.c. supply, using two coils linked by an iron core.
/trænsˈfɔːmə/trans-FOR-muh
🇮🇩transformator
"A transformer steps down the mains voltage for a phone charger."
"Transformator menurunkan tegangan listrik untuk pengisi daya ponsel."
Step-up transformernoun
A transformer that increases voltage (more turns on the secondary coil than the primary).
/step ʌp trænsˈfɔːmə/step-UP trans-FOR-muh
🇮🇩transformator step-up (penaik tegangan)
"A step-up transformer raises voltage for transmission."
"Transformator step-up menaikkan tegangan untuk transmisi."
Step-down transformernoun
A transformer that decreases voltage (fewer turns on the secondary coil than the primary).
/step daʊn trænsˈfɔːmə/step-DOWN trans-FOR-muh
🇮🇩transformator step-down (penurun tegangan)
"A step-down transformer reduces mains voltage to a safe level."
"Transformator step-down menurunkan tegangan listrik ke tingkat yang aman."
National gridnoun
The network of power stations, substations, transformers and cables that distributes electricity across a country.
/ˈnæʃənəl ɡrɪd/NASH-uh-nuhl grid
🇮🇩jaringan listrik nasional
"The national grid delivers electricity to homes across the country."
"Jaringan listrik nasional menyalurkan listrik ke rumah-rumah di seluruh negeri."

9Flashcards

Tap a card to flip it.

StructureTransformer parts
Primary coil, secondary coil, soft iron core (no direct electrical connection between coils).
EquationVp/Vs = Np/Ns
Ratio of voltages = ratio of turns.
RuleStep-up vs step-down
Step-up: Ns > Np (voltage increases). Step-down: Ns < Np (voltage decreases).
WhyHigh-voltage transmission
Same power, higher voltage = lower current = much less energy wasted as heat (P=I²R, so losses fall fast).
SupplementIpVp = IsVs
100%-efficient transformer: power in = power out.
SupplementP = I²R
Cable power loss. Doubling current = 4x the loss.
