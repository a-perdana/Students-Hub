6.2.1 The Sun as a star

Our nearest star up close: what the Sun is made of, what colours of light it actually radiates, and the nuclear fusion furnace at its core that has kept it shining for billions of years.

3Notes

Core

The Sun is a medium-sized star: ~75% hydrogen, ~24% helium, ~1% other elements. It radiates mostly infrared (~50%), visible (~40%) and ultraviolet (~10%).

Earth's ozone layer absorbs most of the more harmful UV.
Supplement

Stable stars are powered by nuclear fusion of hydrogen into helium (4 H nuclei → 1 He nucleus + energy). The Sun's matter is plasma (fully ionised gas); fusion energy takes ~100,000 years to escape the dense core to the surface.

Core ≈ 15,000,000 K; surface (photosphere) ≈ 5800 K.

Sun's mass = 2×1030 kg = 1 solar mass (unit for comparing stars); it holds >99.8% of the Solar System's mass. Earth (~150 million km out) sits in the habitable zone where water can stay liquid.

Full explanations and worked examples: Coursebook Chapter 25.

4Key terms

Pronunciation reference: British Received Pronunciation (RP). /ə/ = the soft "uh" sound in an unstressed syllable — this sound doesn't exist in Bahasa Indonesia.

Stable starnoun
A star that is not collapsing or expanding, because the inward force of gravity is balanced by outward radiation pressure.
/ˈsteɪbəl stɑː/STAY-buhl star
🇮🇩bintang stabil
"The Sun is a stable star, balanced between gravity and radiation pressure."
"Matahari adalah bintang stabil, seimbang antara gravitasi dan tekanan radiasi."
Plasmanoun
A completely ionised gas, so hot that neutral atoms cannot exist; consists of electrons and positively charged nuclei.
/ˈplæzmə/PLAZ-muh
🇮🇩plasma
"The matter inside the Sun exists as plasma."
"Materi di dalam Matahari berbentuk plasma."
Solar massnoun
A unit of mass equal to the mass of the Sun (2×10³⁰ kg), used to compare the masses of other stars.
/ˈsəʊlə mæs/SOH-luh mass
🇮🇩massa surya
"A star of eight solar masses is eight times heavier than the Sun."
"Bintang bermassa delapan massa surya delapan kali lebih berat dari Matahari."
Nuclear fusionnoun
The joining of light nuclei (e.g. hydrogen) to form a heavier nucleus (e.g. helium), releasing energy.
/ˈnjuːkliə ˈfjuːʒən/NYOO-klee-uh FYOO-zhuhn
🇮🇩fusi nuklir
"Nuclear fusion of hydrogen into helium powers the Sun."
"Fusi nuklir hidrogen menjadi helium menggerakkan Matahari."
