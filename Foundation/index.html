How a lesson works

Every lesson follows the same rhythm, so you always know what comes next.

1

Warm-up check

A short quiz before you study. It shows you what the topic is really about — getting things wrong here is the point.

2

Rate yourself

Mark each learning objective red, amber or green. This is your "before" picture.

3

Watch, read, try

Short videos, clear notes, key terms and interactive simulations you can play with.

4

Check again

Theory and practical checks test whether the ideas actually stuck.

5

See your progress

Rate yourself again. The page shows you exactly which objectives improved.

About your progress. Everything you do here is saved privately in your own browser — there is no account and nothing is sent anywhere. That also means your ratings live on one device only: if you clear your browser data or switch to another computer, you will start with a clean slate.
