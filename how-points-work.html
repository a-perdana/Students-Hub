🎯 The short version

Points show how much you practise. They are not money. Your total never goes down, and you cannot spend points on anything. There is no shop, no paid hints and no extra tries to buy. Points move up your level, your tier and your place on the leaderboard.

Each level needs more XP than the last one (1 point = 1 XP). As your level goes up, you move up a tier: Apprentice → Scholar → Mentor → Master → Cambridge Fellow.

Practice never counts toward your school grades. Points are only for fun and for the leaderboard.

⚡ Practice + Daily Challenge

You earn points each time you finish a run on Practice or Daily Challenge. The daily challenge has 5 questions. Everyone in your grade gets the same set, and it starts again at midnight (Jakarta time).

🔥 Daily streak bonuses

Earn points on a day, and your streak goes up by 1. Days follow Jakarta time. If you miss a day, your streak starts again from 1 the next time you earn points.

MilestoneBonus
Day 7 streak+100
Day 30 streak+250

You get each bonus only once. If your streak breaks and you build it again, the bonus does not pay a second time.

🏅 Levels & tiers

Each level needs more XP than the last one. The rule is: to go from Level N to Level N+1, you need 100 + (N−1) × 50 XP. So Level 1 → 2 needs 100 XP, Level 2 → 3 needs 150 XP, and so on. A new tier does not unlock anything. It is a sign of your effort, and it shows on your dashboard and on the leaderboard.

🚫 What points are NOT for

We left these out on purpose. This keeps the hub fair for every student.

✕No paid hints. You can never buy "show me the answer".
✕No spending shop. Points are a score, not a wallet.
✕No penalties. Wrong answers and skipped questions cost zero points.
✕No gifting between students. Your points belong to your account only.
