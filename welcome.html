🛡️ Two rules that matter

🚫 Practice never counts toward your school grades
Practice runs, the daily challenge and the leaderboard are for fun and revision. They earn you points. But they NEVER change your school grades or your report. Wrong answers cost you nothing. So try hard questions and learn from your mistakes, without stress.
🔒 No shop, no buying, no spending
You can earn points, levels and tiers. You cannot spend them, and every avatar style is free for everyone. There is no shop. There are no things to buy. Everyone gets the same chance and the same look options. We did this on purpose, so the hub stays fair for every student.

⭐ How points work — the short version

Here is the very simple version. For the full guide, open How Points Work.

  • Points — you earn them when you finish a practice run or a daily challenge. Correct answers earn more.
  • Level — your level goes up when you collect enough total points. There is a level table on the How Points Work page.
  • Tier — Apprentice → Scholar → Mentor → Master → Cambridge Fellow. Tier moves up with your level.
  • Streak — how many days in a row you earned points. 7 days = +100 bonus. 30 days = +250 bonus. You get each bonus once. Don't break your streak!

📚 Words you will see

Short, plain meanings for the words that show up most often.

Practice Run
A set of questions you start yourself. It earns points. It never counts toward your school grades.
Daily Challenge
5 questions a day in each subject. Everyone in your grade gets the same questions. It starts again at midnight (Jakarta time).
Streak
Days in a row you earned points. If you miss a day, your streak starts again from 1 the next time you practise.
Tier
A label for how far you have come overall: Apprentice, Scholar, Mentor, Master, Cambridge Fellow. It moves up with your level.
Flagged question
If a question looks wrong (a broken image, unclear words), the Eduversal team can flag it and fix it. You will not see this. It happens behind the scenes, so the questions stay fair.
Stuck on something?
If something does not work, or feels wrong, tell your teacher or your school. They can pass it on to the Eduversal team. We fix it for everyone.
